Muryid Steel — manufactured in the [[../Space Geography/Muryid System|Muryid System]] of the same name. The metal is of the highest grade and is precisely the one used for casting the hull plating of space vessels, tank chassis, weapons, and the armor of [[../Military Affairs/Haiduks|Haiduks]] and [[../Military Affairs/Serdiuks|Serdiuks]].
