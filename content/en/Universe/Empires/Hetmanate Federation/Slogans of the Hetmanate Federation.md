# Slogans

![One for All](../../../../Images/Universe/Hetmanate-Federation/Odin_Za_Vsih.jpg)

One for all and all for the Kish.  
Be independent; work together with others.  
My planet is on the frontier; I meet the enemy first.  
Space and Freedom.

## Catchphrases of the Federation's Heroes

> **"Forward, Cossacks! Death walks ahead, but eternal glory walks with us!"**  
> — *Sotnyk Omelian Nechypura, Battle for the Perekop Frontier (Year 320 SE)*

> **"Let their plasma melt our armor — they will never melt our spite! Hold the breach!"**  
> — *Terminator Danylo Morozenko, Defense of the Kodak Orbital Bastion (Year 318 SE)*

> **"Do not look for the landing shuttles, brothers! We leave this rock as victors or as cosmic dust!"**  
> — *Kurinnyi Otaman Taras Honta, Third Siege of the Myurid Red Plains (Year 321 SE)*

> **"They outnumber us ten to one? Excellent! Now we don’t have to waste time aiming!"**  
> — *Haiduk Desiatnyk Lukash Zhuravel, Void Skirmish at Asteroid Khorol (Year 320 SE)*

> **"The void does not forgive cowards, and neither does the Sich! Cut their banner down and shatter their line!"**  
> — *Polkovnyk Severyn Chornobai, Boarding Action on the Imperial Flagship 'Radiant Dawn' (Year 322 SE)*

> **"You may bury me deep beneath the lunar trench, but first let me empty this last magazine!"**  
> — *Serdyuk Vasyl Kryvoshapka, Last Stand at Warp Beacon Azov-9 (Year 319 SE)*

> **"They deployed a capital dreadnought against a single kurin! Cossacks, what greater honor could we ask for?!"**  
> — *Sotnyk Mykola Bohun, Orbital Defense of Wild Field 17 (Year 320 SE)*

> **"Lock your visors and ignite your sabers! Today, we teach the cold stars to fear Cossack steel!"**  
> — *Khorunzhyi Ostap Hostroshablia, The Assault on Iron Gate Nebula (Year 323 SE)*

> **"If the engines fail — ram them! If the cannons burst — board them! While a single Cossack breathes, the Hetmanate never yields!"**  
> — *Otaman Petro Perebyinis, Fleet Clash at the Capricorn Expanse (Year 320 SE)*

> **"Where your boots stand is the border of the Sich! Take one step back, and you owe the Host a planet!"**  
> — *Sotnyk Andriy Vovkulaka, Trench Engagements on Coldmoon Zhovkva (Year 321 SE)*
