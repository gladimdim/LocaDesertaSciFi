# Hetmanate Federation — Visual Reference for AI Art Generation

> Use this document as context in a Gemini notebook to generate concept art, character designs, and scene illustrations for the *Loca Deserta* sci-fi book series.

---

## Universe Overview

The **Loca Deserta** universe is a Ukrainian sci-fi setting spanning three rival interstellar empires. The story focuses on the **Hetmanate Federation (Federatsiya Hetmanatu)** — a Cossack space civilization that blends 17th-century Ukrainian military tradition with far-future technology. Think "Zaporozhian Cossacks in space with powered armor and plasma weapons."

The other two empires are the **Corporate Republic** (American/Western, hyper-capitalist, professional mercenary fleet) and the **Empire of the Sun** (Asian, rigid imperial hierarchy, mass-produced armies with genetic modification). A neutral fourth power, **Jovian Europa**, controls all faster-than-light warp technology from a scientific station on Jupiter's moon Europa.

**Key visual aesthetic:** The Hetmanate is not sleek or polished. Their technology is rugged, smith-forged, battle-worn. Steel is forged by master smiths in the Muryid System. Armor plates are thick slabs, not streamlined curves. Weapons are heavy, brutal, and often hand-crafted. This is a civilization of warriors, colonists, and frontier survivors — not a polished space navy.

## Primary Symbol: The Trident (Tryzub)

The trident is **the** visual identifier of the Hetmanate. It appears everywhere:

- **Silver trident** — on both shoulders of station personnel uniforms
- **Gold trident** — forged onto the nape (back of the neck) of Serdyuk and Terminator powered armor suits
- **Bronze trident** — on the forehead of every bot-horse
- **Crossed spears** — regimental emblem of the "Zbarazh Spears" regiment
- **Falcon silhouette** — secondary symbol, represents attack drones and the "falcon strike" formation
- **Battle banner** (Baghdad-14 campaign award) — yellow field with a bot-horse being struck in the belly by a diving falcon
- **Award badge** — a falcon piercing a bot-horse's belly

**Slogans for banner/insignia compositions:**
- "Kosmos ta Volya" (Space and Freedom)
- "Odin za vsikh i vsi za Kish" (One for all and all for the Kish)
- "Moya planeta z krayu, persym voroga zustrichayu" (My planet is on the edge, I meet the enemy first)

## Military Units — Visual Descriptions

### Terminators (Heavy Space Assault Troopers)

The iconic super-heavy infantry of the Hetmanate. The term comes from the Corporate Republic equivalent — reverse-engineered from captured enemy space marines.

**Suit dimensions:** Up to 4 meters tall. Three layers:
1. **Inner layer:** Synthetic undersuit worn on bare skin (sweat-wicking, temperature regulation)
2. **Middle layer:** Sealed spacesuit for vacuum protection
3. **Outer layer:** Massive forged Muryid-steel armor plates connected by super-strong flexible membrane

**Key visual details:**
- Suit back opens like a clamshell — operator climbs in from behind
- Helmet is a half-sphere: transparent front faceplate, thick-walled back section with cable connectors
- Small auto-cannon mounted on the left shoulder for anti-drone defense
- Gauntlets are artificial manipulators (not hands) — can rotate 360 degrees
- Golden trident forged into the nape of the suit
- Left shoulder pauldron bears the trident-and-crossed-spears emblem
- Battle-worn: scratched paint, dented plates, patched membrane joints
- Serdyuk suits are 3× thicker and heavier than Hayduk suits

**Two sub-types:**
- **Bohatyrs (Assault):** Carry sabers, spears, shields, and short plasma muskets. Lead charges. More ornate equipment, often with personal customization.
- **Riflemen (Fire Support):** Long laser firearms, plasma sabers as sidearms. Operate heavy weapons (machine guns, mortars, plasma cannons).

### Terminator Bruisers (Dubolomy — Close-Combat Specialists)

A variant of Terminators specialized in brutal close-quarters combat. Selected for stronger psyche (and slightly lower intellect — these are the berserkers).

**Visual differences from standard Terminators:**
- Thicker armor plates, slightly larger overall size
- More open visor for wider field of view in melee
- Spare armor plates carried on magnetic mounts on their back
- Their job: "literally tear the enemy apart with their hands"

**Weapons carried:**
- Terminator Shield (large, heavy, rectangular or oval)
- Short Plasma Handgun (sidearm, custom-decorated by each warrior)
- Heavy Plasma Infantry Saber
- Combat Hammer (see below)
- Javelin Spear (see below)
- Grenades and chest-breaker charges
- Fists (armored gauntlets used as weapons)

### Skolot (Super-Heavy Mechanized Armor-Tank)

Among the largest infantry power suits ever developed. A one-person mech-tank hybrid.

**Visual description:**
- Size between a large powered suit and a small tank
- Moves on tank treads **or** mechanical legs (depending on variant)
- Single operator sits in the chest section
- Some variants have additional gunners operating independently
- Armed with: heavy cannons, plasma-spitter, anti-drone mounts
- Firepower second only to a full tank
- Silhouette: bulky, top-heavy, industrial — like a walking pillbox

### Serdyuks (Main Strike Forces)

The primary assault troops. Lighter than Terminators but still in full powered armor.

**Visual description:**
- Fully sealed combat spacesuit, matte finish
- Matte helmet mounted directly on shoulders and chest (no exposed neck)
- 3× heavier armor than Hayduks
- Access to full arsenal: plasma weapons, laser rifles, heavy infantry systems
- First to test all new weapons and equipment
- Gold trident on the nape for higher ranks

### Hayduks (Planetary Defense / Garrison)

Lighter troops guarding planets, stations, and the capital system.

**Visual description:**
- Lighter powered armor than Serdyuks
- Limited arsenal — no heavy weapons
- Use interplanetary aircraft for local patrol
- Silver trident on uniform shoulders
- More utilitarian appearance — these are garrison soldiers, not assault shock troops

### Plastuns (Scouts / Harassers)

Light space infantry specializing in reconnaissance, stealth, and raiding.

**Visual description:**
- No heavy powered armor — wear lighter combat suits
- Greenish-brown anti-radar ponchos (*opanchas*)
- Carry light weapons only
- Stealth-focused: minimal profile, radar-absorbent materials
- Operate behind enemy lines, cut supply lines, sabotage

### Bot-Horses (Mechanized Cavalry)

Four-legged mechanical cyborgs resembling horses. The primary planetary transport for Serdyuks and Hayduks.

**Visual description:**
- Horse-like silhouette with four mechanical legs
- Robotic/cyborg aesthetic — exposed mechanical joints, pistons, wiring
- Powered by a cold fusion engine (requires warp-fuel battery swaps)
- No armor (rarely armored variants exist)
- Small bronze trident on the forehead
- Originally designed by rebel engineers in the Muryid System
- Extremely terrain-adaptive — can cross landscapes that wheeled/tracked vehicles cannot
- Some variants strong enough to carry a full Terminator in armor
- Rider wears Cossack-style clothing over their combat suit, carries plasma weapons from horseback

## Weapons — Visual Descriptions

All weapons are forged by master smiths in the **Muryid System**, the industrial heart of the Federation. Each weapon looks hand-crafted, not mass-produced. Personal weapons are often customized by their owners.

### Combat Hammers

Used primarily by Terminator Bruisers. Three variants:

1. **Plasma Spike Hammer** — One end: a sharp spike. Other end: a blunt striking face that injects plasma on impact, burning through steel and structures. Glowing plasma vents along the hammer head.
2. **Classic Blunt Hammer** — Heavy blunt faces on both ends. Handle pommels vary: steel claw, spear tip, or other attachments. The most brutalist design.
3. **Chekan (Hammer-Axe)** — Hammer on one side, axe blade on the other. Designed for killing lightly armored infantry and breaching tank/IFV armor.

All hammers are large — scaled for 4-meter Terminator suits. Forged steel, visibly heavy, with decorative elements from Muryid smiths.

### Javelin Spear

A spear with a motorized drill-bit tip. Used by Terminators, Serdyuks, and Hayduks.

**Visual details:**
- Long spear shaft with a rotating drill head at the tip
- Can pierce 15–20 cm of standard space armor
- Can mount an AP-15 mine on the tip and be thrown as a javelin
- The AP-15 mine contains 15 kg of melt-substance that sprays 360 degrees on impact (20-meter kill radius)
- Industrial, brutalist design — this is a tool for killing armored targets, not a ceremonial weapon

### Short Plasma Handgun (Plasma Short Samopal)

The standard sidearm for Terminators, Serdyuks, and Hayduks.

**Visual details:**
- Fires 20–50g plasma slugs that burn through power armor and flesh
- Each Terminator customizes theirs — unique engravings, grip modifications, finish
- Single-shot variant: fires the entire plasma flask in one devastating close-range blast
- Design: short-barreled, wide muzzle, with a visible plasma flask/charge pack
- Looks like a sawn-off shotgun crossed with an industrial cutting torch

### Vepr-3 Heavy Infantry Machine Gun

**Visual details:**
- Massive — weighs 300+ kg, requires two soldiers in power armor to carry
- Dual ammo boxes: one for needle explosive rounds (anti-infantry), one for plasma (armor-piercing)
- Can switch between ammo types mid-fire
- Built-in gravitometer for automatic trajectory adjustment
- Remote-operated via AI channel
- Works in vacuum (has oxidizer tanks)
- The name "Vepr" means "Wild Boar" — the weapon looks as brutal as its namesake

### Vepr-1 Stationary Machine Gun

Heavy stationary rapid-fire gun with a protective frontal shield plate for the operator. Fixed emplacement or vehicle-mounted.

### Hornet-42 Flamethrower

A one-meter tube launcher firing 7 kg explosive rounds with 1 kg of plasma dust. Recoilless design — can be fired from bot-horseback. Three-round box magazine. Has "FRAG" mode for proximity airburst detonation.

### AP-15 Anti-Personnel Mine

15 kg of melt-substance that disperses evenly in a 360-degree radius on detonation. Incinerates all infantry within the blast zone.

### Plex-Gel

Viscous fluid sprayed from canisters. Hardens almost instantly to seal hull breaches and armor cracks. Standard equipment for all armored troops. Canisters are worn on belts or webbing.

## Starships and Space Assets

### DARYA-Class Ships

The largest ships in the Hetmanate fleet — over **40 kilometers long** (the flagship *Volyn* is 61 km).

**Visual description:**
- Described as "a giant sausage, inside which everything is stuffed gradually like grapes"
- Exterior wrapped with large guide rails running the length of the hull
- Massive rotating shield plates (up to 10 km radius) ride along these rails to generate warp corridors
- Capacity: 10,000 troops, 20 starships, thousands of orbital ships, thousands of atmospheric flyers
- Ten ships carry one full invasion/defense corps
- Appearance: elongated cylindrical form, industrial, bristling with rails, shield mounts, and docking bays. Not aerodynamic — pure space-only design.

### Smaller Ship Classes

- **"Chumak" class** — heavy cargo starships (named after traditional Ukrainian traders)
- **"Mriya" class** — enormous ice-transport ships (up to 10 km long; "Mriya" means "Dream")
- **Orbital interceptors** — small starfighters for local space defense
- **"Konik" class** — heavy melt-cannon used by Serdyuk mechs

### Atmospheric Flyers

Aircraft for planetary atmosphere only (1000–2000 km range). Military and civilian variants. Used for ground support during invasions and planetary defense. Function over form — these are workhorse craft, not elegant starfighters.

### Orbital Aviation

Can launch from planetary surface to orbit and back. Used for periphery patrol and rapid troop/weapon deployment across planetary hemispheres. Cannot make interplanetary transitions.

### Landing / Assault Capsules

Extremely thick armor. Carry mine-clearing systems for contested landings. Deploy Serdyuks directly into combat zones. Brutalist drop-pod aesthetic.

## Key Locations — Visual Settings

### Zoryana Sich (Star Sich) — Capital

An **artificial asteroid** in the Great Meadow (Velykyi Luh) star system (Virgo supercluster). The system is identical in size and age to the Solar System. The capital is a hollowed-out or constructed asteroid surrounded by satellite stations and planets.

**Visual:** A massive artificial asteroid-station bristling with docking ports, defensive weapons, and shipyards. Surrounded by orbital traffic — warships, cargo haulers, atmospheric flyers. The heart of Cossack power in space. Dark metal, functional architecture, trident emblems visible on major structures.

### Muryid System

The industrial forge-world system. Produces the best armor, weapons, rolled metal, and starship hull plating in the Federation. The birthplace of bot-horses (originally designed by rebel engineers).

**Visual:** Industrial hellscape — orbital forge-stations, asteroid mines, smelting facilities glowing against the black of space. Planet-side: vast smithies, foundries, and weapon-testing ranges. The air quality is terrible. Everything is coated in a fine layer of metal dust.

### Loca Deserta-17 / Dyke Pole-17

The frontier mining colony where the stories take place. A single hive-city on a continent. Mountains to the north. Endless steppe in all directions. One great ocean.

**Visual:**
- **The steppe:** Vast, windswept grasslands under alien skies. Tall grass, rolling hills, the occasional ancient ruin or crashed ship. The steppe is both beautiful and deadly.
- **The hive-city:** A single massive urban structure housing the entire colony population. Industrial mining infrastructure. Protected by Citadel protocol during attacks.
- **Mines:** Deep pit mines extracting nearly the full periodic table from the crust.
- **Skies:** Two suns or an alien star — the light is slightly wrong, slightly alien. Dust storms roll across the plains.
- **Bot-horse riders** silhouetted against the horizon, Cossack patrols crossing the endless grass.

### Great Void of Canis Major

A vast region of the Hetmanate sector with almost no resources — empty, dark, foreboding. Ships cross it only when necessary.

## Hetmanate Soldier Archetypes (For Character Art)

### The Serdyuk (Heavy Assault Trooper)
- 4-meter powered armor suit, matte finish, battle-scarred
- Gold trident on the nape, crossed-spears emblem on the left shoulder
- Half-sphere helmet: transparent faceplate, thick-walled back with cable connectors
- Left shoulder auto-cannon for drone defense
- Carries: plasma rifle or heavy laser, plasma saber at the hip
- Stance: powerful, grounded, heavy — each step is deliberate

### The Terminator Bohatyr (Melee Champion)
- Same 4-meter suit but with personal heraldry — painted details, kill marks, custom engravings
- Carries: a large curved Cossack saber (scaled to Terminator size) or a combat hammer, a large shield, a short plasma musket
- More ornate than standard suits, reflecting personal status and combat honors
- Stance: aggressive, charging, leading from the front

### The Terminator Bruiser (Berserker)
- Thicker armor, more open visor showing a face inside
- Spare armor plates on magnetic back mounts
- Weapons: massive hammer, javelin spear, shield, plasma sidearm
- Battle damage everywhere — these suits take the worst punishment
- Stance: mid-swing, destructive, terrifying

### The Plastun (Scout)
- Human-scale, not giant — wears a form-fitting combat suit with an anti-radar poncho
- Greenish-brown earth tones, camouflage patterns
- Light weapons: a compact laser carbine, a knife, a short plasma sidearm
- Stance: crouched, observing, stealthy

### The Bot-Horse Rider
- A Cossack in lighter combat armor (or traditional Cossack coat over the suit) mounted on a mechanical horse
- The rider carries a short plasma carbine or the Hornet-42 tube launcher
- The bot-horse: mechanical legs, pistons, bronze trident on the forehead, cold fusion glow from the chest
- Stance: dynamic, mid-gallop across the steppe, dust trailing behind

## Enemy Factions (For Battle Scenes)

### Empire of the Sun — Samanaki Terminators
- Blue armored spacesuits with gold trim
- Twin golden serpents winding around the visor edge
- Shoulder plates studded with gold spikes
- Fight with elegant katanas and slim laser rifles
- Genetically modified — slightly alien features
- Walkers: "Tarantulas" — multi-legged spider-like war machines, extremely maneuverable even when missing limbs

### Corporate Republic — Space Marines
- Professional, standardized, mass-produced powered armor
- Clean lines, corporate branding, less personalized than Hetmanate gear
- The "original" that Hetmanate Terminators were reverse-engineered from
- Technologically superior but less brutal

## Key Design Rules for AI Prompts

1. **No sleek sci-fi aesthetic.** The Hetmanate is rough, industrial, forge-made. Everything looks like it was built by a blacksmith, not a factory.
2. **The trident is everywhere.** On foreheads, shoulders, nape armor, banners, ship hulls, drone formations.
3. **Scale matters.** Terminators are 4 meters tall. DARYA ships are 40 km long. Bot-horses are horse-sized. Always show scale reference.
4. **Colors are muted.** Grays, matte blacks, olive-browns, bronze, steel, with gold and silver accents for symbols. No neon. No chrome.
5. **Steppe aesthetic.** The Cossack heritage is always present — traditional coats over combat suits, sabers alongside plasma rifles, bot-horses alongside tanks, ancient symbols on futuristic armor.
6. **Battle-worn, not parade-ready.** Everything is scratched, dented, patched. These are warriors who fight constantly, not a ceremonial guard.
