Sonyachnyk or Sonyachnyky is a mild name for inhabitants of the [[../../Empire of the Sun/Empire of the Sun|Empire of the Sun]]. It is not used officially. It comes from the name "Empire of the Sun." In distant times before the [[../../../History/Great Departure|Great Departure]], it was believed that the sun rose first over their empire. A sonyachnyk is a sunflower, a plant that always tries to orient itself toward the sun and follows its path across the sky. That is the origin of the name.
