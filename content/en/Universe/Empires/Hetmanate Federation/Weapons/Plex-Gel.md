Plex-gel is a viscous liquid that hardens very quickly and fills every crack where it is applied. It is sprayed from canisters. It is used to seal breached space armor after hits or combat damage. It is included in the kit of all armor suits used by [[../Military Affairs/Serdiuks|Serdiuks]] and [[../Military Affairs/Haiduks|Haiduks]].
