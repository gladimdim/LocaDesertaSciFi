**Loca Deserta: Settlers** is a macro-economic space colony builder, inspired by *The Settlers III* and *The Settlers IV*, set in the Loca Deserta universe.

You command a Warp Base dropped into an unclaimed star system: harvest resources, chain production buildings, expand territory with Towers, and colonise planets named after real Ukrainian places (Podillia, Zhovti Vody, Kryvbas). The name is a nod to the *Chumaky* — Ukrainian ox-cart merchants who once hauled salt and goods across the steppe, reimagined here as star-faring haulers.
