# Major Events in History

## Interstellar Transitions

In the year 0 of the star era (2143 old era), the first successful transition into warp was made using the Warp Engine.

The Warp Engine was jointly invented on Jupiter's moon Europa. Europa Jupiteri is a research station where scientists from various empires and planets worked together on the development of Warp technology. For a jump to occur, a large body with gravity (Jupiter) is needed, capable of accelerating a reactor with Lorenzium or Nobelium and compressing space, creating a corridor to another point in space.

The first three interstellar travelers were heads of Warp Engine development departments, representing each of the terrestrial to star empires. Before human launches, there were about 40 successful launches of animals, plants. The journey was programmed so that after 2 days on the other side of warp, the ship would initiate a warp transition back to the Void of Jupiter. Having gathered enough evidence of the safety of warp transition and the synchronization of time (animals returned neither younger nor older), it was decided to send the first interstellar astronauts. The transition was programmed for 3 days near Alpha Centauri, with the ship automatically returning, but, in the case of a successful warp with humans, they were to return within 2 Earth days.

The moment of the ship's launch was broadcasted to all 4 inhabited bodies of the solar system: Earth, Mars, Europa, and the first artificial satellite of Earth. About 20 billion people from all empires witnessed as the ship departed from Europa and headed towards the Void of Jupiter for a warp jump. In the Void itself, astronauts sent the final signal "Let's go" and disappeared from the solar system's radars. The gravitational wave of transition shook the planets, although it was only registered by instruments.

After 2 Earth days, another gravitational wave hit the sensors deployed around the Void. And just a few hours later, Europa received a video signal from the astronauts. The astronomical time of return from warp became the beginning of the countdown for the new Star Era, or SE. Humanity gained access to any star.

## Martian Universal Division and the Tigarden Incident

Every member of the European (Jupiterian) International Research Station (EMIRS) had access to all developments of this laboratory. In the 23rd year of the star era, each of the empires had already founded one colony in the Alpha Centauri system. The search for further systems capable of hosting humans showed that there were quite a few, and systems with valuable minerals were even fewer. Also, warp jumps were quite expensive because Lorenzium and Nobelium are unstable and need to be kept calm only under micro-nuclear explosions. Discussions began among the empires about who has the right to mine asteroids, comets, and planets and to create colonies there.

In the 31st year of the star era, the first armed clash took place during the exploration of the Tigarden Star system. Specifically, on planets suitable for human habitation, Tigarden b and c. The Corporate Republic notified Europe that they had occupied Tigarden B, while the Empire of the Sun notified Europe that the Corporates had attacked their ships in this system and seized control of the warp point, effectively expelling the Sun Empire.

This incident entered the stellar history as the Tigarden Incident. It provoked turmoil in the solar system, with all three empires entering a state of undeclared war. The forces of the Corporates, the Sun Empire, and the Hetmanate headed for Europe of Jupiter to capture control and development of warp transitions. Entry into the gravity zone of Europe meant the first Solar World War, as the Europe of Jupiter zone was demilitarized.

The heads of warp transition development from Europe of Jupiter, namely Danylo Gladkyi, Jack Mo (马云), and Richard Stalman, issued a statement that any further move by any of the empires to capture warp laboratories and the production of warp engines would cause civilization collapse. Therefore, the researchers of the research station would launch the warp engine directly on Europe of Jupiter, causing gravitational collapse and destruction of the moon and the entire station.

Since the presence of any military on Europe of Jupiter was prohibited, and the emperors of all three empires were military men, it was decided to hold a conference on Mars to address the issue of universal division and clarification regarding the acquisition of warp engines.

On July 24, in the 33rd year of the star era, the Martian Universal Division took place. The entire universe was divided into thirty sectors. Accordingly, each sector was a third of a sphere representing the Universe. The division of the universe was done by drawing lots: each of the emperors drew a ticket with the name of a sector. As soon as any of the emperors drew the THIRD ticket with a sector, it automatically became the eternal property of that Empire.

The first three tickets were drawn by Dan Rios, the emperor of the Corporate Republic. They received the upper-right sector. A fiery struggle arose between the Sun Empire and the Hetmanate; each wanted to get the "left" sector because the "lower" sector had a huge pit of the Great Dog where there was almost nothing. Emperor-Koshevoy Taras Velychko and Emperor of the Sun Empire Wang Mang (王莽) could not draw the third ticket with the "left" sector. Five times in a row, each of them drew an already occupied sector. Finally, Wang Mang drew the "left" sector, giving his empire the right to rule there, and the Hetmanate automatically received the "lower" sector. Although the Hetmanate was in despair, the agreement was an agreement.

## Results of the Martian Universal Division of the Year 33 (star epoch)

- Each corresponding sector was granted eternal ownership to the empires.
- The Solar System was to become demilitarized, without the presence of any empire's troops by the 100th SE.
- Each of the empires was obligated to pay a Science Tax to Europe of Jupiter to continue research.
- The empires were to evacuate from the Solar System to their respective sectors.
- The empires were prohibited from developing their warp engines.
- All warp technology belonged to Europe of Jupiter.
- In case of violation of rules or laws or the Martian Universal Division, Europe of Jupiter could disconnect the violator from warp transitions.
- The Solar System became demilitarized, with a population of approximately 15-20 billion.
- Order was maintained by the Peaceful Armies, which directly reported only to the three heads of the laboratory on Europe of Jupiter.
- Each of the empires had a group of representatives on Europe of Jupiter.
- The laws of empires did not apply in the Solar System.
- Europe of Jupiter was prohibited from conducting research or development of any military technologies.
- Europe of Jupiter was prohibited from favoring any of the empires.

It was time for a 200-year continuous expansion of warp stations and space exploration by the three empires. Humanity had conquered the Universe!

The Hetmanate finally evacuated in the 91st SE. Star Sich was founded in the Virgo Supergroup.
The Corporates in the 96th SE. Star Washington was founded in the Centaurus Supergroup.
The Sun Empire in the 87th SE. They founded Star Shanghai in the Pisces-Cetus Supercluster.
