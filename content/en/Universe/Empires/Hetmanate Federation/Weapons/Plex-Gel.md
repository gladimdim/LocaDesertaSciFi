Plex-gel is a viscous liquid that hardens very quickly and fills every crack where it is applied. It is sprayed from canisters. It is used when space armor is depressurized as a result of hits or damage. It is included in the kit of all armor suits used by [[../Military Affairs/Serdiuks|Serdiuks]] and [[../Military Affairs/Haiduks|Haiduks]].
