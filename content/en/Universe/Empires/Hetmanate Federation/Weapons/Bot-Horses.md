Bot-Horses — mechanized cyborgs with four legs that outwardly and functionally resemble horses. Widely used by all forces of the [[../Hetmanate Federation|Hetmanate Federation]]. The primary means of transportation for [[../Military Affairs/Serdiuks|Serdiuks]] and [[../Military Affairs/Haiduks|Haiduks]] across planetary surfaces. A bot-horse is powered by an internal cold fusion engine, which requires replacing the battery containing warp fuel.

![[../../../../Images/Universe/Hetmanate-Federation/Bot_Horse.jpg|443]]

Bot-horses have a great many varieties depending on the purposes of use and the branch of troops employing them.

These horses were first used by [[../Military Affairs/Khartsyzy|Khartsyzy]] rebels in the [[../Space Geography/Muryid System|Muryid System]]. Khartsyzy engineers designed a mechanical cyborg resembling a horse and inflicted a series of defeats on the classic mechanized bands of Haiduks mounted on combat vehicles. Due to their extraordinary adaptability to terrain and cross-country capability, the Khartsyzy on bot-horses could maneuver through areas impassable for conventional APCs and IFVs.

After the suppression of the Muryid Uprising, bot-horses quickly spread across all systems and became part of the jura training curriculum.

![[../../../../Images/Universe/Hetmanate-Federation/Bot_Horse_Rider.jpg|303]]

## Advantages
Can traverse virtually any terrain. Does not require oxygen to operate. Some modifications can carry a heavy [[../Military Affairs/Terminator|Terminator]] on their back.

## Disadvantages
Requires replacement of the cold fusion battery with warp substances such as einsteinium or strontium. Rarely has armor. Vulnerable to kinetic projectiles.
