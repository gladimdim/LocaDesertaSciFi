Loca Deserta is a science-fiction universe rooted in Cossack history and culture. Three rival star empires — the [[Universe/Empires/Hetmanate Federation/Hetmanate Federation|Hetmanate Federation]], the [[Universe/Empires/Empire of the Sun/Empire of the Sun|Empire of the Sun]], and the [[Universe/Empires/Corporate Republic/Corporate Republic|Corporate Republic]] — have divided the galaxy following the [[Universe/History/Martian Division of the World|Martian Division of the Universe]]. Faster-than-light travel is controlled by the neutral scientists of [[Universe/Jovian Europa/Jovian Europa|Jovian Europa]], and the Hetmanate — descendants of Zaporozhian Cossacks — fights to hold its corner of space against imperial aggression.
