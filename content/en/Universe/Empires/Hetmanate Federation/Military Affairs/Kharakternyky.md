The Kharakternyky are an elite semi-mythical secret order within the Cossack ranks. They possess controlled minor genetic and biological mutations that allow them to be stronger, more agile, see in darkness, and read invisible characteristics and intentions of other people. The order also performs surgical military or assassination operations, such as precise elimination of enemies of the Sich, conducting sabotage missions on enemy territory, and deep reconnaissance. Often the exploits conducted by the Kharakternyky are classified, and ordinary people hear only various rumors and fabrications about them, which further enhances their mythical status. The Kharakternyky undergo constant pharmacological programs to maintain their abilities. Thanks to the influence of these drugs, they live 30-40 years longer than ordinary people, practically ceasing to age after 45.

# How to Become a Kharakternik

Through the application of a classified biological program, not every human body can withstand the initial pharmacology course needed to begin transformation into a Kharakternik. The chance of survival after the first injection is one in a hundred million. That is, from a population of one billion men, only ten would survive the first dose. However, during research and development of the Kharakternik program, it was established that prolonged exposure to radiation in the past significantly increases a man's chances of survival during Transformation, when various drugs are introduced into a Cossack's body. Therefore, the order seeks suitable candidates among Cossacks who often spend time in space. The order secretly monitors hundreds of thousands of potential candidates, receiving reports on their health status and how their bodies tolerate various conditions, as well as their intellectual abilities. One of the main conditions is the absence of a family. The most suitable age for transformation is 25-30 years. It is at this age that an anonymous letter of invitation arrives for the candidate. From this point begins the process of Initiation.

# Initiation

Everyone has heard of the Kharakternyky, but no one has seen them. The chances of meeting a representative of this order are as slim as meeting all three emperors in one room in your own kitchen. This is why the chosen initiate is selected in secret. They receive a letter with an invitation to undergo testing and the possibility of joining the order. The letter immediately states that if they accept and do not pass the trial — they will be executed. They have 48 hours to respond. If a candidate refuses, they continue their life without any sanctions from the Kharakternyky. Due to the mythical nature of the order and the spread of various rumors about the severity of punishments for those who displease it, everyone keeps silent.

If a Cossack agrees to try to complete training and initiation, they are sent on assignment. After several transfers at spaceports, they finally arrive at a classified airlock, from where the order's spacecraft delivers them to Heracles' Pillars where they meet the true members of the order. This is where the process of preparing the novice begins. Their former identity completely disappears from all official registers, remaining in the memory of comrades simply as a person who left and vanished.

## Transformation

Upon arrival at Heracles' Pillars at the monastic base, each recruit receives their own cell, where they live in strict asceticism together with other arrivals. They are introduced to active Kharakternyky and told the order's history dating back to its founder — Ivan Sirko, who was the first human to survive the course. The newly arrived have access to almost any information contained in the monastery's library, for there is no return or chance of escape. There will be no information leaks. For the first six months, along with food, a small amount of Serum — a light preparatory form of the Pharmacology Course — enters their body. Since the cost of true Serum with injection is extraordinarily expensive (costing as much as a personal interplanetary starship), the first arrivals are filtered through a weakened version of the course. Usually, from a group of 20-30 arrivals, half are filtered out at this stage. Sudden death, convulsions, bleeding into the brain — corpses are burned in the crematorium and training of the others continues. After six months, the arrivals begin receiving the actual Serum, with dosages constantly increasing. The mixture itself affects the human brain, inducing it into a narcotic state where in one second the brain experiences hundreds of days, analyzing its memory and learning new things. Unlike the effects of a simple narcotic, after the Serum leaves the body, everything the brain managed to learn remains with it, as well as free access of the conscious "clean" brain to all layers of information gained during transformation. It is during withdrawal from the serum that the arrival experiences the greatest suffering. The brain, not yet accustomed to the new mode of operation, reels in a cacophony where each day of torment the person may live through several foreign lives with new information, constantly switching between thoughts, sometimes from neural connections in the head the entire body begins convulsing. This is why arrivals are left alone in their cells after serum injection and opened only after three days. Either retrieving the corpse of a dead man, or a transformed one who has lost twenty kilograms.

This continues for the next six months. From a group of 30 arrivals, 15 die at the trainee stage. Another 7 die after the serum. As a result, only 5-8 Cossacks are initiated into the Kharakternyky in the Salamander caste.

## Werewolves

The most experienced Kharakternyky, who have conducted more than one successful special operation and served in the order for more than one decade. They determine who will receive an initiation letter to recruit into their ranks. They are considered seniors and continue to teach and supervise other Kharakternyky of lower rank. They directly influence the tasks undertaken by their Order. They can also conduct combat operations as a special unit on the most critical areas of war.

## Owls

During service in the order as a Salamander, each Kharakternik gains fame and weight among their peers. Those who served best and most faithfully are promoted to the next rank — Owl. This rank allows independent communication with the Star Sich, providing them with recommendations on further actions. They can pronounce death sentences (through assassination), teach newcomers, and so on.

The combat helmet has the form of an owl with large open black eyes.

## Salamanders

After successful completion of initiation and the full course of serum, the initiated become full members of the order and enter the Salamander rank. They already perform full-fledged missions, serving as advisors to Star System commanders, participating in combat operations to boost the fighting spirit of Cossacks. In those moments when revealing themselves has purpose during battle, they wear combat powered armor [[Terminator|Terminators]] plated with gold. The helmet has the form of a Salamander's head.

## Initiated

Newcomers who survived the first injection of the drug and did not die. They have only begun to unlock the possibilities of their brain, though they do not yet fully control their new abilities.

# Pharmacology

The Serum is developed by the Kharakternyky themselves in their monastery and kept in great secrecy from others.

# Superhuman Abilities

Through the unlocking of their physical capabilities and intellectual abilities, all Kharakternyky possess superhuman powers. They are capable of analyzing thousands of events occurring around them, processing enormous amounts of information arriving from all corners of the universe. By physiological data, they surpass any federation champion, though they never participate in competitions. One of the greatest gifts opened by the serum is the ability to read micro-signals of behavior from people, making them excellent psychologists and interrogators. In rare cases, Kharakternyky gain the gift of seeing in the night.

# Completing the Trial

The final trial before initiation into the Salamander rank takes place on one of the continents of Heracles' Pillars. The initiated is left alone in the middle of a desolate continent with primitive tools — an axe, flint, remnants of food. The sole instruction is "Follow the light." Somewhere in the distance, as if someone is shining a flashlight, a light appears showing the initiated where they should go. This could be a towering mountain or dense jungle. When the Cossack reaches the firefly, they find certain items there that can help them survive in the wilderness: clothing, tools, food, water purifier, and so on. After finding the first firefly, the trial subject is shown the next light and everything begins anew: difficult passage, struggle with the fauna and flora that seeks to kill with each step. The trial subject must demonstrate a hunger for life, willingness to venture into the unknown, and cling to all the knowledge gained during training. This continues until five lights. The initiated, or at this stage usually called the trial subject, reconsiders their entire life, shedding the old and transforming into a new Kharakternik. Usually, everyone completes this stage. Those who could not — remain eaten somewhere in the forest or desert. At the fifth light, Kharakternyky from the Werewolves meet the trial subject and administer the final, consolidating dose of serum. In the morning, the trial subject wakes up already a Salamander and ready for their first missions.

# Heracles' Pillars

The planet on which the Kharakternyky are based takes its name from a region on the Borysthenes River on [[../../../History/Old Earth|Old Earth]] — Heracles' Pillars. These are megalithic structures that rose from the Dnieper, and according to legend, this is where ancient peoples such as the Scythians, Sarmatians, and Greeks originated. The planet's coordinates are strictly classified, with only personal Kharakternik spacecraft having access to the system for flight. The planet has 4 continents and 98% similarity to Old Earth by morphological parameters. Each continent has deserts, steppe zones, jungles, high mountains, and prairies washed by various seas and oceans.

On one of the continents with a steppe-forest zone stands the citadel-monastery where Kharakternyky live, study, and work.
