**Loca Deserta: Settlers** is a free real-time economic strategy (RTS) and space colony builder, heavily inspired by the classic *The Settlers III* and *The Settlers IV*. Set in the Loca Deserta universe, it delivers a deep macro-economic management experience for fans of city builders, production-chain strategy games, and space colonisation sims.

- 🌐 [Play in browser](http://settlers.locadeserta.com/) — free, no sign-up required
- ⬇️ [Download builds (Windows / macOS / Linux)](https://github.com/gladimdim/LocaDeserta_Settlers/releases)

![Loca Deserta Settlers gameplay: warp base and production chains](../../Images/Games/Game_Preview.jpg)

## 🎮 Gameplay & Features

You command a **Warp Base** dropped into an unclaimed star system. Your goal: build a thriving economic colony by harvesting resources, constructing intricate **production chains**, expanding territory with **Towers**, and colonising planets — each offering unique resources and challenges.

This is a true **economic strategy** for genre enthusiasts:
- 🏭 **Deep production chains** — from raw materials to finished goods, Settlers-style
- 🌍 **Planet colonisation** — each planet has unique resources and conditions
- 🗼 **Territorial expansion** — build Towers to claim more land
- 🚀 **Fleet command** — cargo haulers, scouts, builders and warships under one economy

Planet names reference real Ukrainian places: **Podillia**, **Zhovti Vody**, **Kryvbas**, and more.

![Loca Deserta Settlers gameplay: planets and colonisation](../../Images/Games/Game_Preview_2.jpg)

## 🕹️ Who Is This For

If you love **The Settlers**, enjoy **economic strategy games**, **city builders**, or **RTS** titles focused on resource management and logistics — *Loca Deserta: Settlers* is made for you. The game is free, runs directly in your browser, and is available on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
