# Loca Deserta Sci-Fi contributor instructions

## The universe name is never translated

The universe is called **Loca Deserta** in every language except Ukrainian,
where it is **Дике Поле** (with normal Ukrainian declension: Дикого Поля,
Дикому Полю, …).

- Never translate the name: no "Wild Field(s)" in English, no "Wilde Felder"
  in German, and no literal translation in any other language.
- This also covers names built from it: the colony is "Loca Deserta 17"
  (Ukrainian «Дике Поле 17»), and the book series is "Loca Deserta Sci-Fi".
- It applies everywhere: notes in `content/`, site strings in `src/`, the
  scripts in `scripts/`, EPUB configs in `epubObsidianConverter/`, and any new
  translation.

## Art heraldry

- Do not add blue-and-yellow national flags or paired blue/yellow flag bands
  to artwork.
- Do not use the modern Ukrainian coat of arms or a generic three-arrow fork
  as an insignia. Follow the user's ancient princely mark references in
  `output/imagegen/references/princely-mark-primary.png` and
  `output/imagegen/references/princely-marks-examples.png`.
  The primary reference has two broad prongs, a deep U-shaped notch, a tapered
  downward stem and a small separate cross above. Render the central mark in
  subdued metal; omit the reference's blue oval, dotted ring and colored border.
- Do not add decorative rushnyky, embroidered towels, kushaks, sashes,
  embroidered waist belts, tassels or hanging decorative cloth to combat armor.
  Use functional armor plates and equipment attachments.
- Apply this direction consistently to armor, shields, vehicles and other
  faction markings, including image-generation prompts.

## Cossack appearance and akinakes

- Depict adult male Cossacks with moustaches and Cossack hairstyles: a long
  oseledets lock or a top forelock (chub) falling toward the forehead, with
  shaved sides. Vary moustaches, hair, age, facial features and poses between
  characters, including background Cossacks when their features are visible.
- Follow `output/imagegen/references/cossack-hairstyles.png` and
  `output/imagegen/references/cossack-forelock.png`. Use varied Ukrainian
  portrait references for faces; avoid caricatures and identical faces.
- Kharakternyky carry ONE Scythian akinakes per person, sheathed on the
  wearer's anatomical RIGHT thigh. Do not substitute paired Caucasian
  kindjals, modern knives or a left-side mount. The akinakes is a compact
  straight double-edged short sword with a short bilobed guard and a
  transverse pommel; use functional suspension straps.
- Approved character examples are
  `content/Images/Books/Muryid-Uprising/vyhovsky-kharakternyky-station-pencil-v2.png`
  and `content/Images/Books/Muryid-Uprising/vyhovsky-sich-riflemen-tavern-pencil-v2.png`.
  Earlier versions of these scenes were superseded and must not guide hair,
  facial hair or weapons.
- For illustrations in Vyhovsky's diary, retain the approved graphite-pencil
  sketch style on warm off-white paper, with loose hatching and unfinished
  edges, as if drawn by Vyhovsky himself. Apply these appearance and weapon
  requirements explicitly in generation and editing prompts.

## Bot-horse artwork

- Follow `output/imagegen/references/bot-horse-approved-style.png` for bot-horse
  design: recognizable equine proportions, a long segmented neck, smooth curved
  armor panels, slender mechanical legs and compact cameras/sensors clustered
  around an elongated horse-shaped head.
- The spherical heads and exposed box-frame mounts in the v3 cavalry artwork
  were rejected. Do not use those images as design references.
- Keep mounts sturdy enough for their riders but proportionate, without giant
  barrel bodies, oversized block-shaped hooves or spindly insect-like legs.
- Keep the tail as a flexible vox antenna rather than horsehair.
- Cavalry lances have simple straight shafts and small plasma tips, without
  ornate barbs or oversized decorative blades.
