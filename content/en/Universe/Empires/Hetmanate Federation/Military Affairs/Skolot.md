A Skolot is a super-heavy mechanical armor-shell-tank that can hold one or several operators inside, depending on its size. It is one of the largest infantry power suits developed in the Hetmanate Federation. It is armed with heavy cannons, a plasma spitter, and may carry an anti-drone mount. It moves either on tracks or on mechanical legs. It is controlled by one operator seated inside the chest section of the mech. Variants with several additional operators are possible, allowing independent fire from cannons and plasma spitters. In firepower, it is second only to a tank.
