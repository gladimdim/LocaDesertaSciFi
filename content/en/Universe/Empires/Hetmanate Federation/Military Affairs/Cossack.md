Cossack — the general term for any serviceman in the [[../Hetmanate Federation|Hetmanate Federation]]. The designation extends to the entire family if at least one of its members serves in the military. In turn, Cossacks are divided into [[Serdiuks]] and [[Haiduks]]. Regardless of branch or rank, every Cossack stands under the [[Customary Cossack Laws]].
