[[Колонізація Федерації Гетьманату]]

Founded in 1861 (282 years before the Star Epoch), it includes all European countries, North Africa, Anatolia, and the Middle East.


Nickname: Hetmanate

Governed by the Council of the Sich. Elected by the vote of the elders.


Political System	Federation
Genetic Technologies	Support for natural abilities
Social Structure	Segregation based on abilities
Advantages	Autonomy of palanks, a great inclination towards startups and conditionally independent existence of starostas and settlements, extraordinary mobility of the population.
Military Affairs	All citizens are military obligated. Only the class of Cossacks (serdyuks, haydamaks, haiduki, plastuns), Janissaries, and Mujahideen perform permanent service. During the war, a punitive Hetman is elected with absolute power.
Disadvantages	Extraordinary people are not respected, it is impossible to rise above all due to general uniformity. Therefore, there is weak development of fundamental science. Due to the collegial decisions of the elders and the Council of the Sich, decisions are often blocked. The system works effectively only during full-scale wars.
Governing Body	Council of the Sich. Elected from the elders.
Social Elevator	 At 12 years old, all citizens undergo a classification test for abilities and receive a classifier number for life.
Citizen Freedoms	 Citizens freely travel the galaxy, can change government and chieftains.
Science	Fundamental science is not very developed. Representatives of other empires are often hired to work in their scientific centers. They quickly adapt to new knowledge developed by others and can independently improve it.
Economy	Production of organic food, heavy industry, manufacturing of various weapons
Mottos	One for all and all for Kish.
    Be independent, work together with others.
    My planet from the edge, meet the enemy first.
    Space and Freedom.
