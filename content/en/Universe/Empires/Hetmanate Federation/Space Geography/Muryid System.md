The heart of the arms industry of the [[../Hetmanate Federation|Hetmanate Federation]]. It forges the best armor, weapons, rolled metal, and even hull plating for spacecraft.

The star system consists of six planets, extraordinarily rich in metal deposits of all types. Only three of them are inhabited. Two billion citizens of the [[../Hetmanate Federation|Hetmanate Federation]] permanently reside there, most of whom originate from Asia Minor on [[../../../History/Old Earth|Old Earth]] and are ethnically descended from Arab lands.

The system is renowned for its [[../Miscellany/Muryid Steel|Muryid Steel]], which is used in the manufacture of ships, armor, vehicles, and colony stations.

## Muryid Uprising

In 315 [[../../../History/Stellar Era|Stellar Era]], the [[../Events/Muryid Uprising|Muryid Uprising]] took place in the Muryid System — an insurrection that nearly destabilized the entire Hetmanate. Due to the immense value and importance of [[../Miscellany/Muryid Steel|Muryid Steel]], the Hetmanate committed massive [[../Military Affairs/Haiduks|Haiduk]] forces to suppress the rebellion.
